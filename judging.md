# Judging criteria: NASA Space Apps Cairo 2026

Pasted by the team on 2026-11-13.

1. **Impact:** a meaningful real-world problem; who benefits and how; positive impact on people, science, society, Earth or space exploration.
   *Why does this project matter, and who will benefit from it?*
2. **Creativity:** an original idea or fresh perspective; an innovative approach or improvement; inventive use of available tech and resources.
   *What makes our solution different or innovative?*
3. **Validity:** sound scientific and technical principles; can realistically work; demonstrated through evidence, testing, a prototype, model or demo; claims backed by reliable information and results.
   *Does our solution actually work, and can we justify how it works?*
4. **Relevance:** clearly addresses the selected NASA challenge's objectives; uses relevant NASA data meaningfully; shows how NASA data supports the solution; responds to the actual problem described.
   *Does our project genuinely solve the selected challenge, and how does NASA data contribute to it?*
5. **Presentation:** clearly explain the problem and why it matters; describe the solution and how it works; present approach, methodology and results in an organized way; use clear visuals, demos, prototypes or supporting materials; communicate value and potential impact.
   *Can someone understand our problem, solution, and its importance through our presentation and submitted materials?*

> A strong project is not just a creative idea. It should address a meaningful problem, offer a relevant and feasible solution, make meaningful use of NASA resources, demonstrate its potential value, and communicate it clearly. Focus on all five criteria.

## Before submitting
- [ ] Selected and addressed a NASA challenge.
- [ ] Meaningfully incorporated relevant NASA Open Data and Resources.
- [ ] Developed a clear and feasible proposed solution.
- [ ] Prepared the required project description and supporting materials.
- [ ] Checked that all submitted links are accessible.
- [ ] Followed the official submission instructions, including the AI-use disclosure and content requirements.
